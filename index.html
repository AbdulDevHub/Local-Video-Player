    About Local Video Player

    Everything a desktop player does, without installing one

    • history

      Continue watching

      Your place and speed are saved for every file. One click brings you back to where you stopped.

    • playlist_play

      Playlists & folders

      Drop a whole season or a folder of lectures. Episodes play in order, one after another.

    • subtitles

      Subtitles

      SRT and VTT, matched by file name. Fix out-of-sync timing and resize the text.

    • speed

      Speed control

      0.1× to 16× in fine steps, plus one-key presets for 1.5×, 2×, 2.5×, 3× and 4×.

    • volume_up

      500% volume boost

      Make quiet recordings and lectures loud enough to hear.

    • repeat

      A-B loop & frame steps

      Loop a section to practise, step frame by frame and save screenshots.

    • fit_screen

      Zoom & stretch

      Fill the screen by cropping or stretching, and fine-tune it with arrow keys.

    • wifi_off

      Offline & private

      No uploads, accounts or ads. Install it as an app and it works without internet.

    Questions

    Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

    No. Files are played directly from your device by your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and there are no accounts, ads or tracking.

    Which file formats can it play?

    Anything your browser can decode: usually MP4 (H.264, H.265 on supported hardware, AV1), WebM, MKV, MOV, OGG, MP3, M4A, WAV and FLAC. Chrome and Edge support the most formats.

    How do I add subtitles?

    Drop an .srt or .vtt file onto the player, or drop it together with the video. Subtitles with the same file name as the video are matched automatically. You can shift the timing and change the text size from the subtitles menu.

    Does it remember where I stopped?

    Yes. Your position and speed are saved in your browser, and partly watched videos appear in the Continue Watching shelf. In Chrome and Edge one click reopens the file; in other browsers you pick the file again and it resumes.

    Does it work offline?

    Yes. After your first visit the player works without an internet connection, and you can install it as an app. It is also available as a Chrome extension.
